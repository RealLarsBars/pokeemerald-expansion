# Tone: serious, in the spirit of the books. Wit belongs to a few clever characters only.

@ gText_Birch_Welcome
Hi! Sorry to keep you waiting. The ravens are restless tonight, and so am I.

Welcome to HOENN, a realm of eight great houses and one empty-hearted Throne.

My name is BIRCH.

I am a Maester of the old order. I keep the records of every POKéMON in the realm, and of every lord who has tried to rule them.

@ gText_Birch_MainSpeech
This realm is shared with creatures called POKéMON. The lords say they own them. The POKéMON have never once agreed.

Some fight beside us. Some carry our letters. A few, the old songs say, could drown the world or set it burning.

The great houses have bled each other for a hundred years over who sits the POKéMON THRONE. Steel, fire, sea and stone. Every one of them has buried sons.

And now two new claimants rise. One swears the land is his by right. One swears the sea will take it all.

Winter is not coming to HOENN. Something worse is. The scrolls call it the Drowning and the Burning, and they do not say who survives.

I have spent my life reading those scrolls, and I still do not know how the story ends. That is why I need you.
